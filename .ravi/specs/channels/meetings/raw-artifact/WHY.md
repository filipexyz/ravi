# Meeting Raw Artifact / WHY

`meet.md` is the durable handoff from a live meeting back to the requesting
Ravi session. It preserves what was observed: metadata, participants,
transcript, chat, Ravi output, media refs, diagnostics, and artifact lineage.

The raw artifact deliberately avoids summaries, decisions, and action items so
the consuming agent can reason from provenance instead of inheriting hidden
interpretation from the meeting runtime.
