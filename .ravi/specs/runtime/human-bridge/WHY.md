# Why

A transport identity cannot represent the human requesting a private agent turn.
The Console already knows that human and decides their access. A signed, short
lived delegation lets an installation enforce those decisions without a second
permission policy. Durable receipts and scoped event cursors handle uncertain
network outcomes without duplicating execution or disclosing another person's
history. The listener is explicitly enabled; existing integrations keep their
current auth and runtime behavior.

## Desktop Teams

The original Desktop includes human/agent teams and visible handoffs. A separate
explicit roster preserves that feature without changing privacy of existing
conversations. Requester-bound leases prevent a better-privileged team member
from silently lending authority to another person's work. Typed installation
controls preserve the original inspector without a generic remote shell.
