# Contacts / WHY

Contacts are Ravi's public CRM and relationship surface for people and
organizations. Raw phone numbers, WhatsApp LIDs, Telegram ids, emails, and chat
ids are transport evidence, not the canonical human model.

The contact domain keeps identity resolution, contact policy, CRM state, chat
membership, and agent identity separate. This prevents groups from becoming
people, agents from becoming human contacts, and hidden contacts from leaking
through raw identity lookup or CRM projections.
