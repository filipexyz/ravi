# Why decisions are independent

A question can stop a thread before a task exists. A fake task would conflate work tracking with deciding. The generic ledger owns history; two explicit origin adapters own access and consumer behavior. There is no registry of arbitrary workflow types.

The managed lifecycle id distinguishes the work, canonical chat identifies its conversation, and session key pins the runtime waiting for the answer. Raw transport ids stay in the existing Slack adapter.

Exact-once provider execution cannot be inferred from a broker ACK. A durable admission receipt prevents ordinary duplicates; ambiguous admission stops for operator evidence instead of silently losing or repeating work. The deliberate recovery command is narrower than allowing a worker to assert its own receipt.

Thread close refuses every open question because even a nonblocking question would otherwise disappear behind a completed card. Tasks retain their independent lifecycle and parked fold.
