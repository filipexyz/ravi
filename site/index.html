01 ASK

00, Ravi/ 00 — RAVI

AGENTS THATWORK TOGETHERIN YOUR SLACK.

Ask in Slack for what you need, the way you'd ask a colleague. A team of AI agents plans it with you, builds the forms and trackers your team will use, and passes the work along until it's done. Ravi runs on a computer or server you control and joins your Slack as a bot you own.

Someone technical sets it up once. After that, your team works in Slack and a browser.

02, Build/ 02 — BUILD

FIRST THE TABLE, THEN THE PAGE.

The dev agent builds two things: the table that keeps every purchase request (Ravi Bases) and the page your team opens to file them (Ravi Pages). Nobody on your team writes code.

Skip this scene ↓

  5. 05When it's done, dev tells reception, the front desk, which posts the link in #office.

09, For Builders/ 09 — FOR BUILDERS

BUILD YOUR
OWN APP
ON RAVI.

Want your own app? Your developers can build a dashboard, an iPhone app or a Mac app on your agents and tables, with SDKs for TypeScript, Swift and Dart generated from the commands your agents use.

await ravi.sessions.send("ops", "Summarize the open requests.", { agent: "ops", wait: true })
Copied

One line from your app asks ops, the same agent your team talks to in Slack, and waits for its answer.

ZERO DRIFT

The SDK code in Ravi's repository is generated from the same list of commands your agents use. If the two ever differ by even one byte, the automated check fails.

TypeScript: @ravi-os/sdk on npm. Swift (for iPhone and Mac) and Dart: from the GitHub repository. About 35 commands stay on the command line only, and many results aren't fully typed yet. The connection your apps use is off until you turn it on, listens only on your own machine until you allow more, and each app needs a key you create for it. To reach it from a phone, set up your own secure tunnel. Apps act as the Ravi account signed in on that machine; they don't sign in each user.

10, Install/ 10 — INSTALL

TRY IT IN YOUR SLACK.

Setup is a one-time job for someone comfortable with a terminal. After that, your team only uses Slack and a browser.

Not the one who sets things up? Send this page to whoever is.

  1. 01

    Install Ravi

    $bun add -g ravi.bot
    Copied

    Needs Bun and Node.js. Then run ravi setup: it installs what Ravi needs, asks for your Claude login or Anthropic API key, and starts Ravi. Then switch your first agent to Claude; the README shows how. (Setup asks its questions in Portuguese for now.)

  2. 02

    Connect your Slack

    Create a Slack app for your workspace and connect it to Ravi. You'll need permission to add an app to your Slack workspace. There's no one-click install yet; the README on GitHub links to the step-by-step guide. On Linux, Ravi keeps your Slack keys in Vault, so you'll need one.

  3. 03

    Ask in Slack

    Give a channel to your first agent and choose what it may do, such as building pages and posting in Slack. Then ask, the way you'd ask a colleague. To publish pages and tables, sign in to Ravi once.

Your agents run on your own computer or server and use Claude over the internet, so they work while that computer is on. Pages and tables are hosted by Ravi online; pages open at ravi.page. Your agents use your own Claude login or Anthropic API key, so AI usage counts there. Plan cards need agents that run on Claude. Task cards need Work Object Previews turned on in your Slack app.
